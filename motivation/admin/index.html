Questions & Answers

Are these the same students as Campaigns?

Yes. Names, phones and class assignments use the shared school records. Other open program tabs may need to refresh.

How do I move between students?

Open a profile, hold Shift, use ↑ ↓ for students or ← → for classes, then release Shift. Escape cancels the preview. Alt+1 returns to search.

How are exam points calculated?

The highest minimum score reached determines the award. Rules are saved with the exam event; later rule edits do not rewrite earlier awards.

What happens with a wrong-class card?

The card is blocked. An administrator must reopen it with a reason. Reusing a redeemed card is denied.

When are delayed card points revealed?

At midnight on the next day in the school timezone. Manual points and exam awards are immediate.

Quick start

  1. Create a program and term under Program Settings.
  2. Set subject score rules and choose the active program above.
  3. Open a student to add points, record an exam or redeem a card.
  4. Use Documents for IDs, reports and reward-card batches.
  5. Use Reports to inspect accepted and denied actions.
  6. Set a display schedule and playlist, then open the projector.
